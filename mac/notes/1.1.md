## CursorApp Helper 1.1

- A shorter menu: “Hide on This Network” is gone, and the version shows without the build number.
- The pointer follows your finger more precisely and no longer freezes, then jumps, after a pause or a Wi‑Fi hiccup.

Requires macOS 14 or later and Accessibility access.
