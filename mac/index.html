Ready in three steps.

Install Helper on your Mac and open CursorApp on your iPhone. Both devices need the same Wi-Fi network.

  1. Install Helper

    Open the download. Drag CursorApp Helper into Applications, then open it.

  2. Allow control

    Turn on CursorApp Helper in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

  3. Pair your iPhone

    Choose “Pair a Phone…” in the Helper menu. Tap “Search” in CursorApp on your iPhone and choose your Mac.

Helper stays in your menu bar. Open at Login and Check for Updates… are in its menu.

My Mac doesn’t appear

Check that both devices are on the same network. Open Helper → “Pair a Phone…”. If macOS asks about the local network, click “Allow”.

Connected, but the cursor won’t move

Turn on CursorApp Helper in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

My Mac stopped responding after an update

In Accessibility, remove CursorApp Helper with the “−” button, then add it again from Applications. This also applies when switching from a self-built copy.

Login and updates

You’ll find “Open at Login” and “Check for Updates…” in the Helper menu.

Uninstall Helper

Quit Helper from its menu and delete it from Applications. If needed, remove it in System Settings → General → Login Items. The keychain item is called “Vl.CursorAppHelper”.

Signing and download verification

Helper is signed with Developer ID “Vladislav Sukhov (Y3SRRD5QMG)” and notarized by Apple. Every update is signed with EdDSA and verified before unpacking.
